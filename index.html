About

I am a PhD candidate supervised by Matthias Bethge, part of IMPRS-IS and the ELLIS PhD Program. I hold an M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering and Management from Technische Universität Berlin, with a focus on machine learning, data science and data engineering.

Research

My research is on the evaluation and training of AI tutors — judging a tutor by the progress a learner makes over an interaction rather than by how pedagogical an individual response looks.

Outreach

Alongside my research I am involved in educational outreach. I teach AI workshops in middle and high schools with KI macht Schule, to help build the technical and ethical understanding a generation needs to use these tools responsibly. Previously I volunteered with Starcode e.V., giving coding classes for middle and high school girls, and with the Berliner Lesepaten, practicing reading with elementary school children.
