About

I am a PhD candidate supervised by Matthias Bethge, part of the International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems (IMPRS-IS) and a member of the ELLIS PhD Program. I hold a background in Industrial Engineering and Management from Technische Universität Berlin.

Research

My research is on the evaluation and training of AI tutors — judging a tutor by the progress a learner makes over an interaction rather than by how pedagogical an individual response looks.
